**Topic Covered**: `WHERE`

## Problem Statement

Which presidents were no more than 10% older than their spouse(s) at the time of marriage? Display president name, president age at marriage as `pr_age`, spouse name, and spouse age as `sp_age` for marriages where the ratio `CAST(pr_age AS DECIMAL) / sp_age` is between 1.0 and 1.10 (inclusive). Sort by pres_name ascending, then by spouse_name ascending.

## Expected Output Columns

| pres_name | pr_age | spouse_name | sp_age |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
